About Me

I am a mechanical engineer and PhD researcher working at the intersection of building performance simulation, computational modelling and digital building technologies.

My research combines physics-based building simulation with uncertainty quantification, surrogate modelling, explainable methods and optimisation to support robust energy and retrofit decisions.
